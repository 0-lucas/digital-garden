# What is it?

In [[Discrete Mathematics]], *relations* are used to evidence **some sort of relationship between two [[Sets|sets]]**. Given two non-empty [[Sets|sets]] $A$ and $B$, the *relation* $R$ from $A$ to $B$ is a *subset* of $A \times B$. Then, the *relation* $R$ consists of *ordered pairs* $(a,b)$, where $a \in A$ and $b \in B$. If $(a,b)\in R$, it's considered to be **related to**.
However, **$a$ being related to $b$ does not necessarily imply that $b$ is related to $a$**.

A *relation* $R$, by itself, can be considered as a *kind of 'rule'* between $a$ and $b$. If $a,b$ are related by $R$, that means they **obey the same rule $R$**, and can be written as $xRy$.
