# What is it?

In [[Machine Learning]] and [[Deep Learning]] models, the *type of environment* which these models are **either trained or act in**, can be **decisive to its performance**. Models trained in past data of a environment that is **rapidly changing** will **never be able to perform well in it**, once that characteristics of this environment is not the same from the training data.
This is also **one of the reason** of the [[Distribution Shift]] problem in **deployed models**.

To better understand how to handle problems with *unpredictable and dynamic environments*, and perform well in them, **one must adjust its strategy to it**.
