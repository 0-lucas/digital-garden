# What is it?

*Conditional [[Probability]]* can be understood as the probability of an event happening, **assuming that another event has already happened**.

*The probability of A, given B*, expressed as  $P (A|B)$, is the occurrence of event *A* is **conditioned to the previous occurrence** of *B*, which assumes $P(B) > 0$. This reduces the [[Sample Space|sample space]] for the problem, given that **all events** in the [[Sample Space|sample space]] are **equally probable**.
