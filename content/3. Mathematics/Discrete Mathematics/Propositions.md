# What is it?

In [[Discrete Mathematics]], a *proposition* is a statement that is declared as either **true** or **false**. These are given the notation of *variables*, like $p, q, r, s \dots$ to notate each *proposition*, e.g. :

- $r = (2+2 = 5)$
- $p = \text{'Today is going to rain.'}$
- $q = \text{'It rains every single thursday.'}$

___
The use of [[Logical Operators]] **enables complex logic** between different *propositions*, creating other *propositions* from a preexisting one. Given the implication $p \rightarrow q$, other three related implications can be defined:

- ##### Converse
	Defined as $q \rightarrow p$.

- ##### Inverse
	Defined as $\neg{p} \rightarrow \neg{q}$.

- ##### Contrapositive
	Defined as $\neg{q} \rightarrow \bar{p}$.
