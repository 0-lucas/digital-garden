*Discrete Mathematics* is the branch of  [[Mathematics]] which focuses on studying structures that are **countable**, **distinct** and **separate**. *Discrete Math* **does not bother itself** with continuous functions or decimal real numbers.

Therefore, it's focus is on using logical statements to solve real-world problems based on *discrete structures*, which are [[Sets|sets]], *functions*, *sequences*, *relations* and [[Graphs]].

It's widely used in [[Computer Science]] and [[Software Engineering]] to create logic inside software.
