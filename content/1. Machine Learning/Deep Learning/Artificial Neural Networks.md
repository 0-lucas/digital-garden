# What is it?

*Artificial neural networks* are a *software approximation of a biological human brain*, which tries to **mimic the process which neurons communicate to each other** to process information. *ANNs* are at the very core of [[Deep Learning]], and provide **much more versatile and flexible models** than traditional [[Machine Learning]] modelling. 
___
# How does it work?

Proposed by [Warren McCulloch](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Warren_Sturgis_McCulloch) and [Walter Pitts](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Walter_Pitts) in the paper *"A Logical Calculus of Ideas Immanent in Nervous Activity"*, the biological nerve cell  served as the basis for the creation of the *artificial neuron* structure, which is basically a **gate which receives one or more binary input and outputs a single binary**.
